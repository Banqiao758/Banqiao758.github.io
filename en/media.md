support a specific claim only when corroborated by the

primary sources; uncorroborated claims are explicitly flagged as such. See
<a href="{{ '/en/methodology/' | relative_url }}">Sources &amp; Methodology</a>

## Entry format

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Title | Report title |
| Outlet/Source | Newspaper, radio station, TV station, or news agency |
| Publish date | Date the report was published |
| Summary | A brief, non-verbatim description of the report's content |
| Corroboration | Whether it's corroborated by this site's Official Archive or other primary sources; uncorroborated content must be flagged |
| Rights status | Typically "cited only," or a short fair-use quotation of a specific fact where needed (see Sources &amp; Methodology); this site does not reproduce the full text or extended passages |
