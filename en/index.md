<section class="intro">
  <p>
    In early August 1975, Typhoon Nina stalled over the upper Huai River basin and
    produced extreme, sustained rainfall over southern Henan Province. Over the
    following days the Banqiao Dam and a number of other reservoirs in the
    Zhumadian region failed. The event is documented in Chinese and international
    engineering and hydrology literature as among the most severe dam-failure
    disasters on record, though its scale, causes, and consequences went largely
    unpublicized outside specialist literature for many years afterward.
  </p>
  <p>
    Much of what circulates about this event online repeats secondhand claims
    &mdash; some accurate, many exaggerated, distorted, or shaped by political
    agendas on more than one side &mdash; without traceable sourcing. This
    project takes the opposite approach: every claim published here is built
    outward from identifiable primary material &mdash; engineering records,
    meteorological and hydrological data, government and technical archives, and
    first-hand testimony &mdash; with its provenance stated. Where the evidence
    is incomplete or contested, that is stated too, rather than smoothed over.
    See <a href="{{ '/en/methodology/' | relative_url }}">Sources &amp; Methodology</a>.
  </p>
</section>
