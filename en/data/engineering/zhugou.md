The figures below are drawn from 《河南省水库基本资料汇编》(Henan Province
Reservoir Basic Data Compilation), compiled by the Henan Provincial
Revolutionary Committee Water Resources Bureau (internal circulation,
January 1974), pp. 186&ndash;187. Zhugou is much smaller than Banqiao,
Shimantan, Boshan, or Suyahu, and the source's entry format for it differs
slightly (using "Location / River system" instead of "Construction site /
River," and including a flood-regulation table by recurrence interval).

## Basic information

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Location | Zhugou, Queshan County |
| River system | Qintou River (溱头河), a tributary of the Ru River |
| Controlled catchment area | 28.5 km&sup2; |
| Area flooded | 600 mu |
| Completion date | May 1970 |
| Downstream conditions | Zhugou Commune's seat is 1 km downstream |
