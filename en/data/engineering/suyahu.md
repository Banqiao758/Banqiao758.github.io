The figures below are drawn from 《河南省水库基本资料汇编》(Henan Province
Reservoir Basic Data Compilation), compiled by the Henan Provincial
Revolutionary Committee Water Resources Bureau (internal circulation,
January 1974), pp. 44&ndash;49. Suyahu Reservoir held despite exceeding its
design storage during the "75.8" flood &mdash; this corroborates this site's
<a href="{{ '/en/archive/1975-henan-early-august-report/' | relative_url }}">Henan Provincial Water Resources Bureau's early-August report</a>,
which records Runan County party secretary Yuan Shengchen organizing
military and civilian crews to reinforce the dam. The source copy carries
handwritten annotations beside some printed figures (all higher than the
printed values, likely added later by the book's owner; the date of
annotation is unknown) &mdash; both are given below where they differ.

## Basic information

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| River | Ru River (汝河) |
| Construction site | Runan County (汝南县) |
| Controlled catchment area | 3,398 km&sup2; (annotated as 3,918 km&sup2;) |
| Full-compensation elevation | 56.5 m |
| Channel safe discharge | 1,400 m&sup3;/s (annotated as 1,800 m&sup3;/s) |
| Construction date | 1958 |
| Farmland flooded | 241,000 mu |
| Population relocated | 45,730 |
