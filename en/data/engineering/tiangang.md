Tiangang Reservoir sits immediately downstream of Shimantan Reservoir and
failed in turn after Shimantan breached on August 8, 1975. Reconstruction
was decided in 1997 and completed in December 2002. The 1958-original
figures are extracted from 《舞钢市武功乡志》(Wugang City Wugong Township
Gazetteer), pp. 109&ndash;111, as is the 2002-reconstruction data. A separate
1974 record of the original 1958 structure, from 河南省革命委员会水利局编
《河南省水库基本资料汇编》(internal circulation, January 1974), pp.
174&ndash;175, corroborates the 1958 figures below (see "1974 source
supplement" further down).

## Engineering specifications (1958 original vs. 2002 reconstruction)

| Parameter | 1958 original | 2002 reconstruction |
|---|---|---|
| Dam type | Homogeneous earth dam | Homogeneous earth dam |
| Main dam length | 330 m | 305 m |
| Auxiliary dam length | 450 m | 1,435 m |
| Main dam max height | 16.5 m | 19.1 m |
| Dam crest elevation | 90.5 m | *not stated* |
| Total reservoir capacity | *not stated (per 《河南省水库基本资料汇编》: design-level capacity 24.2M m&sup3;, check-level capacity 27.0M m&sup3;, dead storage 6.5M m&sup3; &mdash; see the supplement below)* | 31.76M m&sup3; |
| Flood-control design standard | 1-in-100-year design, 1-in-500-year check | 1-in-50-year design, 1-in-1000-year check |
| Spillway max discharge | 780 m&sup3;/s | 4,020 m&sup3;/s |
| Controlled catchment area | 75 km&sup2; | 305 km&sup2; |
| Design irrigation area | 112,000 mu | *not stated* |
| Total investment | *not stated* | 43M yuan (central government 14M, Henan province 14.5M, Pingdingshan municipality 4M, Wugang city 10.5M) |
| Key dates | Started Jan 1958, completed April 1958 | Reconstruction decided Feb 16, 1997; completed Dec 2002 |

*Figures are taken directly from 《舞钢市武功乡志》as stated, without
independent verification or unit conversion by this project. The large
discrepancy in controlled catchment area between the two periods (75
km&sup2; vs. 305 km&sup2;) may reflect a redefinition of the catchment
boundary rather than a physical change, but this hasn't been confirmed.*

## 1974 source supplement (original 1958 data)

The figures below are drawn from 《河南省水库基本资料汇编》, pp. 174&ndash;175,
a pre-failure (i.e. before the August 1975 breach) record that corroborates
the "1958 original" column above: controlled catchment area (75 km&sup2;),
main dam length (330 m), max dam height (16.5 m), dam crest elevation
(90.5 m), spillway max discharge (780 m&sup3;/s), and design irrigation
area (112,000 mu) all match. This source also clarifies the tiered
breakdown of total capacity, which the gazetteer excerpt alone left
ambiguous ("the relationship between the two is unclear"):

| Level | Elevation (m) | Corresponding capacity (10,000 m&sup3;) |
|---|---|---|
| Dead level | 85.3 | 650 |
| Normal (beneficial) level | 89.0 | 2,300 |
| Design level (1-in-100-year) | 89.33 | 2,420 |
| Check level (1-in-500-year) | 90.0 | 2,700 |

This shows the previously-noted "check-level capacity of 6.5M m&sup3;" was
actually the dead-storage figure, not the check-level capacity (2.7M
m&sup3;) &mdash; corrected here accordingly.

Other figures: annual runoff 56.61M m&sup3;; design flood 705 m&sup3;/s;
check flood 792 m&sup3;/s; wave-wall crest elevation 91.5 m; dam crest
width 6 m; earth/rock/sand 500,000 m&sup3;; masonry 25,000 m&sup3;;
rebar/concrete 4,200 m&sup3;. Downstream conditions: the Beijing-Guangzhou
railway.

*One date discrepancy is worth noting: 《舞钢市武功乡志》 records the
completion date as "April 1958," while 《河南省水库基本资料汇编》 records
"December 1958." Both are as stated in their respective published
sources; this project cannot determine which is correct and presents both
here as-is.*
